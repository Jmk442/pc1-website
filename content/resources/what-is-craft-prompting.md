---
title: What is C.R.A.F.T. prompting?
description: C.R.A.F.T. is a five-part method for writing clearer AI prompts: Context, Role, Action, Format and Tone.
---

C.R.A.F.T. is a repeatable way to brief an AI system.

- **Context** sets the situation and the limits.
- **Role** tells the model what kind of helper to be.
- **Action** names the task.
- **Format** shapes the output so you can use it.
- **Tone** matches the voice to the reader.

It is not a magic formula. It is a checklist that makes your intent visible.

If you already know what you mean, C.R.A.F.T. is a way to say it in an order a model can follow. PromptCraft 1 walks through each element, then shows the same method in study, work, and professional settings.
