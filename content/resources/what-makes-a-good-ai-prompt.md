---
title: What makes a good AI prompt?
description: A good prompt is a complete instruction: it tells the model the situation, the job, and the shape of the answer.
---

A prompt is just an instruction. The quality of the result usually follows the quality of that instruction.

A weak prompt leaves the model to guess the audience, the purpose, the length, and the voice. A stronger prompt answers those questions before generation starts.

You do not need a clever trick. You need enough information that a capable person could do the task without coming back to ask what you meant.

That usually means:

- who this is for
- what you want produced
- how the answer should be structured
- what to avoid

PromptCraft 1 teaches this as C.R.A.F.T. — Context, Role, Action, Format and Tone. The letters are a memory aid. The skill is deciding those five things before you ask.
