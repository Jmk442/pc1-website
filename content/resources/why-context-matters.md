---
title: Why does context matter when using AI?
description: Without context, a model has to guess. A short situation note is often the difference between a usable answer and another retry.
---

Generative AI does not know your classroom, your workplace, or your reader unless you say so.

If you write “summarise this,” the model has to invent an audience, a length, and a purpose. That guess may be wrong. Then you retry. Then you retry again.

Context is the missing brief. It can be short:

- who will read this
- what they already know
- what must not be included
- any length or format limits

You are not giving the model extra decoration. You are reducing the number of things it has to invent.

PromptCraft 1 treats Context as the first move in C.R.A.F.T. because most weak prompts fail there, before Role or Tone ever come into it.
